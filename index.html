What is Armenia?

Armenia is an ancient country that has existed for thousands of years. Its capital is Yerevan.

Three fun facts about Armenia:

  1. Early Christianity

    Armenia was the first country in the entire world to proclaim Christianity as the state religion.

  2. Influential Figures

    Armenia's history is renowned for figures such as St. Gregory the Illuminator, St. Gregory of Narek, and St. Nerses Shnorhali.

  3. Mountainous Geography

    Armenia's geography includes many mountains. The highest one is Mt. Aragats, which is 13,435 feet high.
